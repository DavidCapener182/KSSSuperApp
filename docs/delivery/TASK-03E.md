# TASK-03E proposal — Office Onboarding workspace, queue and cover

**Status:** approved by David and implemented in the dedicated synthetic development project. This is the original design proposal; see `TASK-03E-REPORT.md` for executed evidence and current limitations.

## 1. Outcome and scope

Give authorised Office staff a useful Onboarding landing page and a bounded way to continue a starter's work when its original owner is absent. Deliver a scoped dashboard, searchable team queue, case ownership transfer with history, finite named-case cover, and honest presentation of cancelled work. Reuse the existing case, requirement, private Document, review, Task, Profile, controlled Terms and audit records. This is an Office workflow task; it does not complete Core KSS induction or make a person compliant or deployable.

Preserve the accepted Staff A Template V2 case, all five fulfilled requirements and their exact business records. Its sixth requirement remains `NOT_CONNECTED`; 03E demonstrations should use additional synthetic cases for reassignment/cancellation rather than altering Staff A's accepted 5-of-6 proof. The actual LMS and usable interface remain unknown. Training needs its own approval even if its provider becomes known during 03E planning or implementation.

## 2. Existing constraints found in the repository

Today `onboarding_cases.owner_person_id` is fixed to the original creator by `private.guard_onboarding_case()`. Onboarding RLS, `readOnboardingCase()` and private Profile/SIA RLS use that owner. A linked personnel `DocumentRequest.requester_person_id` is also the only ordinary Office reader/reviewer, and `DOCUMENT_REVIEW` Tasks are assigned to that requester. `private.guard_task()`, Task RLS and `resolveTask()` require the Task assignee and request requester to match. The guarded RTW/SIA/Identity verification operations and evidence-link guards compare the request requester with the case owner. Cancellation currently blocks further upload/review but does not give a linked Open Task an honest terminal state. The Office case list is limited to 50 rows and shows little workflow summary; there is no team scope, search or shared queue. These are coupled rules, so changing the case owner column alone would strand requests and Tasks or require unsafe broad Office access.

The proposed change preserves `created_by_person_id`, the original `DocumentRequest.requester_person_id`, immutable evidence versions, review actors and completed Task actors as historical facts. It introduces explicit current authority for *onboarding-linked* work. Generic personnel-document requests retain their existing requester rule unless a later task separately approves delegation for them. SiteAssignment never grants onboarding authority.

## 3. Recommended bounded team and authority model

Create a small `onboarding_teams` identity and effective-dated `onboarding_team_memberships` for active `OFFICE_ADMIN` People. A case has one current `team_id` and one current `owner_person_id`; team membership alone permits a **minimal triage row** for active team cases, not detailed case/profile/evidence/acknowledgement records or file bytes. A new case is assigned to a team explicitly by an authorised Office creator who belongs to it; an existing owned synthetic case may be enrolled once through a guarded, attributable administrative operation. Company-level cases can have `site_id = NULL`; Site is context, never the team permission key. Do not create a universal department or permissions engine.

Only `SUPER_ADMIN` initially creates teams and grants/revokes/sets membership dates. A narrowly granted team coordinator permission may allow a named active member to reassign cases within that team; the permission is separate from ordinary membership and cannot be self-granted. The current active case owner may reassign their own case. `SUPER_ADMIN` can perform an audited reassignment/cover intervention when the owner is absent. Ordinary membership cannot change role assignments, publisher grants or staff acknowledgements.

The access predicate for any Office action is the intersection of: active Office role, explicit active team membership, exact case assignment/finite cover, active case state, action-specific capability, and self-action prohibitions. Team membership is an eligibility gate; case ownership or named-case cover supplies detail/action scope. A coordinator grant supplies reassignment authority, not private evidence access. A user with multiple roles cannot verify their own requirement or review their own evidence. Super Admin oversight remains explicit and audited.

### Queue projection versus detailed case

The team queue may expose the minimum triage fields: case ID, synthetic starter display name, intended role, template/version, contextual Site label, case state, count of currently complete requirements, high-level blocker/next actor, current owner display name, and last meaningful activity time. A row may say **Identity Evidence — Office action needed** without exposing document names, review comments, contact details, home address, submitted revisions, version IDs or bytes. Use a tightly scoped server/database projection to supply names because ordinary `people` and Profile RLS do not permit team-wide reads. Do not grant table-wide Person/Profile SELECT to make the queue work.

The owner, active named-case cover holder and authorised Super Admin may read the full case subject to the existing action-specific rules. For cover, private Profile/revision and RTW/SIA/Identity metadata/bytes become available only for the exact named case and action needed, while the grant and recipient's Office role/membership are active; no global document-audience widening. Audit privileged reads as already required. Historical completed work retains original actors. Team triage users cannot follow a guessed case URL to obtain detail or bytes.

## 4. Controlled reassignment and temporary cover

**Permanent reassignment:** current authorised owner, scoped team coordinator, or Super Admin selects an eligible active Office member of the same team and gives a required controlled reason (for example cover handover, workload balance or leaver). A guarded operation locks the case and relevant Open Tasks, verifies both the actor and recipient at action time, changes only current ownership, and appends an immutable `onboarding_case_owner_changes` business-history row containing case, old/new owner, actor, reason and database time. The case's original creator and historical requester remain unchanged. Reassignment to the same owner is an idempotent no-op or predictable conflict; no silent history entry. A future cross-team transfer needs separately specified authority and is outside the normal 03E path.

**Temporary cover:** a finite, named-case grant records case, original owner, covering Office Person, effective start/end, grantor, reason, revocation and timestamps. Recommend a maximum initial duration of 14 calendar days for the synthetic proof; David should approve that limit. It never becomes generic impersonation or permanent ownership. The recipient must be an active member of the case team with their own active Office role; actions remain within the recipient's role/capabilities and the case's approved onboarding scope. The grantor must be the current authorised owner or Super Admin. Cover expiry or revocation immediately removes case detail, related private bytes, and action authority without relying on a scheduled cleanup. If the owner loses their Office role or the case leaves the team/cancels, cover ceases to authorise action. The grant does not expand controlled-document publication or Staff acknowledgement rights.

For the first implementation, cover grants **named-case detail and normal owner-equivalent onboarding actions** while active, including an exact linked document review if the covering user meets the reviewer and self-review rules. It does not change `owner_person_id`, original requester or Task assignee. The cover holder sees pending actions in a clearly labelled **Covering** My Work section sourced from the same Open Task IDs; the current owner remains the accountable assignee until a real reassignment. An actual review records the covering Person as reviewer and resolves the existing Task from the authoritative review event. This avoids a duplicate Task and makes the action provenance clear. Cover cannot be chained or re-delegated.

## 5. Document, verification and Task transition

An onboarding-linked request must be identified from its exact RTW/Identity case-requirement link or SIA submission link; title, Site and requester alone are insufficient. Keep the original requester's Person ID as historical provenance. For an active case, the current eligible owner or active named-case cover holder becomes the permitted ordinary Office reviewer for that exact linked request/version, subject to no self-review and all existing latest-version, acceptance/rejection and Storage safeguards. An Office user who only sees the team queue gets no document metadata or bytes. Generic document requests continue to use the original requester. Update server checks, document metadata RLS, Storage byte policies, review RPC/guards, Task source readability and relevant onboarding verification guards consistently; direct authenticated RPC and object access must agree with the UI.

On **permanent reassignment**, an existing Open `DOCUMENT_REVIEW` Task for the case keeps its ID, source version, created time and state. A guarded transaction changes its current assignee to the new case owner and appends an immutable assignee-history row with old/new owner, actor, reason and time. Its uniqueness by `(source_kind, source_id)` remains. Any later Staff replacement submission creates a new Task for the then-current case owner. A completed Task is never reassigned; its reviewer/completion event and historical assignee remain intact. The Task guard and reconciliation adapter must be changed narrowly so they use the current linked case authority for onboarding sources and retain the original requester behaviour for unrelated documents. A failed transition rolls back the case reassignment atomically, so the case cannot move while its Open review remains stranded.

For **temporary cover**, the Open Task stays assigned to the owner and is shown as covering work only to an active cover holder. The actual reviewer Person is recorded by `document_reviews`. The existing Task becomes `DONE` only when that exact review decision exists; cover grant, Task visibility or a UI click cannot resolve the business decision.

RTW, SIA and Identity requirement-verification guards must replace the temporary `requester_person_id = current case owner` assumption with two independent checks: the immutable request is correctly linked to the case/Person and original authorised requester, and the acting verifier has *current* owner or active named-case cover authority plus active Office role, or audited Super Admin authority. Exact accepted version and SIA revision/expiry protections remain. A reassignment cannot reverify or rewrite prior decisions. Staff self-verification remains denied even with another role.

Staff-only controlled Terms access/acknowledgement is unchanged. The new owner may see its permitted status; neither owner nor cover can acknowledge for Staff. Existing publisher capability remains separate from team/ownership authority.

### Cancelled work

Add only one Task terminal state, `CANCELLED`, for an Open `DOCUMENT_REVIEW` Task linked to a case cancelled before its source review decision. Record cancellation time and exact case cancellation event/cause. A guarded case-cancellation transaction moves only those Open linked Tasks to `CANCELLED` and audits the transition; already `DONE` Tasks remain `DONE`. Do not call them Done, delete them, or fabricate `document_reviews`. Cancelled Tasks are absent from actionable My Work and live queue counts but available in authorised history with the reason **Case cancelled**. Existing cancelled-case upload/review guards remain. Inspect older synthetic cancelled cases and any Open linked Tasks before deciding on a small explicit reconciliation; no blanket historical mutation. The case itself remains historical and cannot be reassigned, covered, reviewed or verified normally after cancellation.

## 6. Office workspace and Staff experience

Make `/onboarding` the Office landing page while Staff keeps **My Onboarding**. The Office view should have authorised summary counts (Active starters, My cases, Needs Office action, Waiting for Staff, Blocked), followed by practical views **My cases**, **Team queue**, **Needs Office action**, **Waiting for Staff**, **Blocked**, and **Cancelled / history**. Active and cancelled work are separate by default. Every count and list comes from the same scoped case predicate, with server-side pagination; a 50-row truncation must not produce misleading totals.

Queue rows show starter, intended role, template/version, current owner, progress as **N of 6**, primary blocker, next actor and last meaningful activity. Derive progress/blocker/next actor from existing requirement, evidence, review, profile submission, controlled acknowledgement, Task and training-provider facts. Where several actions coexist, show one deterministic primary next action plus a count/list of other blockers; do not persist a competing editable workflow status. Last activity uses a whitelisted meaningful business-event timestamp (case start, submission, review, verification, acknowledgement, owner change, cancellation), not mere page views or generic audit noise. Search/filter by authorised starter name, case state, owner, next actor/blocker, version and contextual Site. Counts, search and pagination must all apply the same scope before returning results, including zero-result behaviour; never fetch a global list then filter in the browser.

Desktop uses a readable queue table with one clear next action and an accessible reassignment/cover dialog. At 390px use cards and compact filters without horizontal page overflow. Provide empty, loading, error and stale-action feedback, including an explanation when a cover expires mid-session. The case detail keeps evidence review separate from requirement verification. My Work remains the source of specific accountable Task actions; the dashboard represents cases and their overall state, and the **Covering** section identifies delegated Open Tasks without cloning them. Do not create one generic Task for every case.

Staff My Onboarding continues to show its six requirements, next action and feedback. Office staffing and cover details need not appear to Staff. Reassignment cannot alter Staff's Person ID, case ID, evidence links, submissions, acknowledgement or progress. If a contact label is later useful, use a role/team contact rather than exposing an individual Office worker's private details in 03E.
