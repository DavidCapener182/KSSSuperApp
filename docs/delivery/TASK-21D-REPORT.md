## Approved boundary delivered

The first slice adds two separate sections to the accepted Service Delivery detail: **Commitments** and **Changes**. A Commitment can only be `PROPOSED_UNVERIFIED`, then `ENDED` or `WITHDRAWN`; no 21D RPC or table state can confirm contractual authority. Cards explicitly say “Proposed / source not contractually verified”. The seven approved management categories, bounded factual description, active Office owner, applicability dates, reasoned owner reassignment and immutable revision history are present.

Changes target one exact `site_services.id`, its baseline revision and exact `site_service_events.id`. Event and operational-document targets are disabled in the database and UI. TASK-19A had not been formally accepted at verification; no operational-document adapter or 19A mutation was added. A proposal has an exact Europe/London requested effective instant, controlled summary/reason and optional exact related 21D change. The management lifecycle is `PROPOSED → UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED | REJECTED | WITHDRAWN`. Approval remains visibly **awaiting source application** and does not write Site Service or any other source module. `APPLIED` requires an already successful, later, exact 08A Site Service event advancing the approved baseline by one revision on the same bound Service. `NOT_APPLIED` records a distinct factual outcome. One current application outcome is enforced. Later source revision drift preserves the original event link and displays **Source changed since this application**.

Super Admin alone administers finite, named `SERVICE_CHANGE_PROPOSER`, `SERVICE_CHANGE_APPROVER` and `SERVICE_CHANGE_RECORDER` grants under an exact Service Delivery. Recipients must be active Office Admin people. Super Admin role alone cannot propose or approve. An actor with both grants cannot approve their own proposal. Recorder authority does not grant 08A source authority. Office/Super management read remains under the exact 21B Service Delivery → historical Site Client Link → Site Service relationship; Operations, Staff and direct base-table access remain denied. A source deep link uses its owning module's independent access check.

The server stores management summaries and exact source IDs/revisions, without contract text, private SOP content, contacts, Incident narrative, personnel evidence, prices, rates or correspondence. No Service Delivery RPC writes Site Service, demand, allocations, Event, Documents, Assets, Contacts, CRM, Attendance or Worked Time.
